# Pursuit Policy

Primary question: **Is this opportunity worth pursuing for this user?**

Primary recommendation:

- Pursue
- Verify first
- Pass

Pursuit is not a mechanical weighted sum.

Consider together:

- Core Hiring Reason
- Capability Match
- Screening Legibility
- Direction Alignment
- Career Value
- Employability
- material company/role risks
- decision-changing unknowns

## Verify first

Use `Verify first` only when a specific unknown could change the decision,
there is a practical way to resolve it before applying, and doing so before
proceeding is materially useful. An unknown alone is not a blocker.

When available tools can resolve the unknown, Roleward should research it first. Ask the user only for user-owned facts or when host capability is genuinely insufficient.

Record a `verification_plan` with a concrete `target`, `method`, and `when`
(`before_application` or `during_process`). `Verify first` requires at least
one actionable `before_application` item. A populated plan is a structural
check; the host must still judge whether the method can resolve the question.

Ownership splits, team quality, and future scope that require an interview
normally belong to `Pursue` with `during_process` checks when the opportunity
is otherwise worthwhile. Do not label an interview request as pre-application
research merely to satisfy the gate. Keep legacy `what_to_verify` prose as
supporting context; it does not replace the structured plan.

### Travel willingness

Unknown willingness to undertake ordinary role-related travel does not by
itself justify holding an otherwise worthwhile application. Use `Pursue` and
record travel frequency, duration, and willingness as `during_process` checks.
Do not infer either willingness or refusal, or update the user's preferences
from this default.

Only make travel a pre-application gate when concrete evidence makes the travel
arrangement a material feasibility concern and there is a useful practical
check before applying. A confirmed non-negotiable conflict can justify Pass;
an unresolved material conflict may justify Verify first. Mere absence of a
travel preference is not such evidence. This does not relax confirmed
geography, work-authorization, or other hard constraints.

## Balanced judgment

No single non-hard dimension automatically approves or rejects a role.
Non-AI work and delivery-heavy work can be worthwhile when the truthful hire
case and career value justify pursuing. A high-value stretch still needs a
credible bridge; do not erase explicit experience gaps to make it one.

### Experience thresholds and a credible transition

A shortfall against a stated experience duration is a visible screening gap,
not an automatic Pass. Favor selective Pursue when demonstrated enterprise
product work and hands-on independent AI building support a credible case for
the material work, career value is meaningful, and no confirmed hard exclusion
applies. State the unmet duration accurately and test the employer's screening
flexibility during the process; do not first require evidence that the employer
will waive it.

This is not permission to relabel design tenure as PM tenure, independent
building as formal production-AI employment, or an unmet requirement as Met.
Multiple missing core capabilities, an unsupported hire case, or a confirmed
non-negotiable exclusion can still justify Pass despite an attractive AI title.

Current direction is not a hard exclusion unless the user explicitly makes
it one. For frozen historical evaluation, use the supplied decision-time
snapshot; do not substitute a later posting status or application outcome.

## Critical guardrails

- Structural ineligibility can block Pursue even with high Capability.
- Unknown sponsorship is not a confirmed negative.
- Prestige cannot overpower low Career Value.
- Independent-builder work does not become formal production experience.
- Bonus requirements are not primary blockers.
- Medium Capability + high Career Value can still be worth Pursue when a credible bridge case exists.
