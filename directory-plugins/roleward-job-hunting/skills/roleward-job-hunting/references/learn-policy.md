# Track and Learn Policy

Track explicit lifecycle updates conversationally.

Outcome status and outcome reason are separate. An unreasoned rejection remains unknown.

Learning levels:

- Weak Observation
- Emerging Pattern
- Confirmed Signal

One outcome or one Pass reason is never enough to become causal market truth or a global preference.

Repeated independent observations may form a preference hypothesis. User confirmation is required before promotion to a persistent confirmed preference.

Unconfirmed repeated inferred signals may only:

- break ties between otherwise similar opportunities;
- slightly reorder secondary results;
- determine which preference hypothesis is worth asking about next.

They may not:

- exclude an otherwise strong opportunity;
- override confirmed constraints/preferences;
- determine Pursue/Pass by themselves;
- become outward factual claims.
